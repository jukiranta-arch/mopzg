Amazon.com, captures from 2026-09-28 (about 3,600 books, 76 searches). BSR = Best Sellers Rank on the day captured. Each lesson names its evidence, so later data can overturn it.

## Patterns

1. **"For men" usually fails.** It failed in grief journals, word search, nostalgia puzzles and memory books. The buyers there are women, buying for themselves or as caregivers. It works in two places:
   - Christian devotionals: *One Minute with God for Men*, BSR 74.
   - Keepsake journals bought by children for their fathers: *Dad's Story*, BSR 4,904.
2. **Many direct books with no sales is not a gap.** It means the concept has been tried and failed. The scorer flags this as TRIED AND FAILED.
3. **Copycat waves arrive fast.** Monochrome Christmas coloring had 7 of 16 books under 60 days old. The 1960s word searches included several books under 6 months old with no sales.
4. **Places where newcomers win right now.** These are books under 6 months old selling at least 3/day with fewer than 150 reviews:
   - Murder mystery puzzle books: *Kill Grid Vol. 1*, 84 days old, BSR 721, 44 reviews.
   - Seasonal adult coloring with a twist, large print or monochrome: *Large Print Halloween Coloring Book*, 47 days old, BSR 4,509, 2 reviews.
   - Bible study workbooks: *Bible Study Workbook for Beginners*, 23 days old, BSR 8,651.
   - Executive function workbooks for kids and teens: BSR 12,725 and 17,965.
   - Memory activity workbooks for seniors, including a volume 2 at 71 days old and BSR 26,923.
5. **Seasonal niches have to be judged by last season.** In September, Christmas books look dead. Check their December rank history on Keepa.
