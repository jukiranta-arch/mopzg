## Corrections to the positioning brief

1. **The mastermind is not new to name-list books.** The *Find The Killer* series (*The Superyacht Murders*, *The Ski Lodge Murders*, …: five 555-suspect cases and a mastermind) has it. The brief listed that series' sales (0.1/day per book) but wrongly said no name-list book had a mastermind. Its sales rank today is about 1,100,000.
2. **Checking an answer without spoilers is not new either.**
   - *The Killer Bought a Ticket*: "CHECK YOUR SOLUTION (written in a way to avoid accidental spoils)".
   - *The Killer Was On The Guest List*, third edition: the solution is printed in a cipher.
   - *The Sealed Verdict*: "The Unspoilable Murder Mystery Puzzle Book".
   - Our version checks every case as you go, which is a smaller difference than the brief claimed.
3. **What is still ours, as far as these 188 listings show:**
   - one cozy small town and one story across five linked cases, each case 2,800–3,700 names;
   - cut-paper silhouette illustrations;
   - a strong cover-and-title package.

- **Sellers:** a hook title and a professional cover.

## Description

The winners' pattern:
1. a capitalised numbers line;
2. a short story hook;
3. a capitalised "HOW IT WORKS" section with points;
4. "Perfect for…";
5. a closing question.

They run 900–3,500 characters, and 63 of the 77 first-captured books have A+ content.
