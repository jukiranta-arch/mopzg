# A picture style that doesn't look like ChatGPT

Juho's verdict on the first pictures: "super generic chatgpt art". He is right.

## Why it looked generic

- **The prompt led straight to the default.** "Black ink pen-and-ink drawing, mid-century book illustration, fine cross-hatching" is ChatGPT's most common black-and-white look: a finely hatched digital engraving.
  - Every surface carries the same density of detail.
  - Light glows.
  - Faces are smooth and similar.
- **Competitors already use it.** The Killer Was On The Guest List's illustrations are exactly this look, so it can't set us apart either.
- **Readers say what gives AI art away** (Rolling Stone, the Canadian school-library survey, cover-design blogs):
  - a polished, slightly uncanny finish;
  - faces and hands;
  - garbled small lettering;
  - sameness.
- **What our own reviews say:** of 93 competitor reviews, the 3 that mention AI are about the writing and the puzzle feeling lazy. None mentions pictures. So the risk is the general "this is AI slop" impression, and generic art feeds it.

## How the image model is steered (OpenAI's own prompting guide)

The guide is in the OpenAI cookbook, *GPT Image Generation Models Prompting Guide*, read from GitHub because developers.openai.com is blocked here.
- **Name the medium concretely:** its materials, texture and physical behaviour. Generic adjectives don't steer it.
- **Avoid words that imply studio polish.** For a natural look, ask for real texture and imperfection.
- **Structure the prompt in labelled parts** (scene, subject, details, constraints), and state exclusions explicitly.
- **Lettering:** put it in quotes or capitals, and spell hard words letter by letter.
- **Style transfer:** give a reference image and say what to take from it (palette, texture, marks) and what not to.
- **Consistency:** anchor on one approved image and repeat the critical details each time.
- **Iterate in small single changes,** repeating what must stay the same.

**ChatGPT itself:**
- Images 2.5 (8 September 2026) is the same model on web, desktop, mobile and the Work app.
- Plus users can choose **Thinking**, which follows instructions better.
- Edits accept up to 10 reference images.
- Output goes up to 2160p.

## What makes a picture read as made by a person

1. **The marks of a real medium:** carved lines, scissor-cut edges, flat printed inks with slight misregistration.
2. **Simplification:** one focal point and big shapes. AI's mistakes live in small detail, so simplicity hides them.
3. **Few or no faces:** profiles, silhouettes, figures from behind.
4. **No smooth gradients or glow.** Real print has flat inks. Smooth grey ramps are the clearest machine signature, and they also print muddy on KDP's black ink.

## Three candidate styles, all different from every competitor sample

| Style | Why it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Linocut print** | Hand-carved, bold, warm; common on indie bookshop posters and literary covers. Two inks print perfectly. | Can get heavy; faces crude |
| **B. 1950s screen-printed poster** (black, one grey, white) | American small-town nostalgia, like the National Park and travel posters; flat shapes and no faces. | Can look like vector clip art if too smooth |
| **C. Cut-paper silhouette** | Mystery made visual: every figure is a shadow, a suspect. Hides all faces. Very elegant. | Less atmosphere; lettering must be cut out of a sign |

- **The competitors:** Guest List uses hatched ink, Eliminate grey realism, Never Checked In photo-realism, Autumn a leaf icon. None uses any of these three.
- **The test:** Juho makes the bookstore in all three styles (prompts in `books/juniper-falls/art/PROMPTS.md`). Each goes into the real opening page, and we choose on the page, not on the screen.

## What the build does once a style is chosen

- `design.ART_INKS` snaps every picture to the style's flat inks.
  - **2 inks** (black and white) for linocut or silhouette.
  - **3 inks** (black, a 35% grey and white) for the poster.
- The breaks between inks come from each picture's own tones (Otsu's method), then stray specks are cleaned away.
- **Why it helps:**
  - any leftover AI gradients are gone;
  - all 14 pictures share the same inks;
  - the grey is a flat tint well above KDP's 10% minimum.
- Until the style is chosen, pictures keep their own tones; pale ones are darkened to one average tone.
