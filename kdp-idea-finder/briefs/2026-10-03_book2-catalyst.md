# Book 2: finding a catalyst (2026-10-03)

Each candidate catalyst has to pass three tests:
1. **Pain:** buyers in our niche complain about it in reviews.
2. **Proof elsewhere:** the feature already sells in a neighbouring niche.
3. **Absence:** no name-list book has it.

**Sources:**
- 123 reviews of 19 name-list books (amazon.co.uk). Signed out, so about 5–13 reviews per book.
- 27 captured amazon.com searches.
- 269 autocomplete suggestions.

| Theme | Reviews | Of them 1–3★ |

## Candidates

### B. Large print, for older solvers: passes all three tests

- **Pain** (17 reviews):
  - "It is 12 point font and I had to separate almost every page from the binding"
  - "The font is SO small"
  - "over 100 pages of tiny font"
  - "Names ran into the fold"
  - "pages are so thin highlighters … seep through"
- **Proof elsewhere:**
  - "large print puzzle books for seniors" has a median sales rank of **1,413** in its top 16. That is the strongest demand of any search we have captured; "murder mystery puzzle book" is 3,007.
  - The top books there have 500–9,000 reviews each: KAPPA, Brain Games, Merriam-Webster.
  - Related searches:
    - "activity books for seniors": 4,917
    - "brain games for seniors large print": 7,591
    - "dementia activities for seniors": 9,492
- **Absence:**
  - None of the top results for "large print murder mystery puzzle book" is a large-print name-list book.
  - The only large-print mystery puzzle books we found are **word searches**: *Murder Mystery Word Search: 100 Large Print* (rank 42,782) and *Cozy Autumn Word Search*.
  - The top 9 of "large print puzzle books for seniors" contain no mystery puzzle at all.
- **Fits the format:** large print means fewer names per case, about 1,000–1,500 instead of 3,700. That also answers the "tedious" complaint. Shorter cases can stay fair and still need thought.
- **Buyers and gifts:** older solvers, plus their children buying gifts (one review: "I got this book as a gift from my daughter").
- **Costs:** about 180–220 pages.
  - Print cost: $1.00 + 0.012 × 200 = $3.40.
  - At $12.99 the royalty is about $4.39.

### A. Clues you have to earn (Murdle-style deduction before elimination): plausible

- **Pain:** reviews saying it was too easy or just scanning, about 11 of them in the 1–3★ group. For example: "no logic or critical thinking required", "clues are mostly about the letters".
- **Proof elsewhere:** *Murdle* and *Murdoku* rank 10–63.
- **Absence:** not clean. Murdle-style grid books are crowded:
  - *Whodunit?* (32,140)
  - *DELICTA*
  - *The Crimson Library Murder* (774)
  - *Montague Island Mysteries*

  The hybrid itself was not found, but the deduction half is a crowded field.

## Recommendation

Book 2 is **a large-print cozy find-the-killer book for older solvers**, by Nora Clewes:
- big type (14–16 pt names), wide gutters and thick rules;
- shorter, fair cases;
- a story;
- the spoiler-free check.

Optionally, add A's earned clues sparingly, as a small twist rather than the headline.

## Correction (same day): the absence test was wrong

The first absence check looked only at the **top 16 results** of a few searches. When every captured title was checked (6,659 titles, all result pages of every search, plus the database), both remaining candidates turned out to be already done many times over.

### B. Large-print find-the-killer: about 20 books already exist

- **LARGE PRINT KILLER MYSTERY PUZZLE BOOK** series, four books: 3–13 ratings; one has a sales rank of 1,585,719.
- **THE KILLER NEVER LEFT THE …** large-print series, four or more books: 0–3 ratings; one has a rank of 124,153.
- **Name the Culprit** large-print series, five books, including *The Layover Murders: … Three Linked Murder Cases*: 0–1 ratings.
- *Who Killed Grandma? (Large Print Mystery Club)*: 2 ratings.
- *Murder at the Book Club: A Cozy Large-Print Find-the-Killer Puzzle*: 1 rating.
- *Cross Out the Suspects … in True Large Print*, *The Killer Borrowed a Book … in Large Print*, *Find the Killer: The Bitter Vigil (Large Print)*, *The Lucky Ticket (LARGE PRINT)*, *Clear Sally Catch the Killer (Large Print Edition)*: 0–1 ratings.

All of them came out June–September 2026, and none sells. The large-print senior market buys word searches and brain games, not this format.

### A. Elimination plus earned puzzles: crowded too

- *The Telephone Book Killer Strikes Again!* ("Decode the Adverts, Eliminate the Names")
- *The Hartwell Reunion* ("Cross 40 Suspects Down to One Killer Through 40 Ciphers, Riddles & Logic Puzzles")
- *The Killer Isn't at the Wedding* (1,200 suspects and four hidden ciphers)
- *The Puzzle Master's Casebook* (an elimination book with chained sudoku, crosswords and word searches)
- *THE KILLER ISN'T WHO YOU THINK* (elimination with word searches and cryptograms)

All have 0–3 ratings.

### What this means

- The name-list niche had a wave of launches in June–September 2026, and every obvious twist has already been tried.
- What sells is execution and the package (*Alice*, *Never Checked In*, *Mr Darcy*), not a feature.
- **Fix to the method:** the absence test now always scans every captured title, never just the top 16.

**Revised plan:**
1. Wait 2–4 weeks for *Who Sent the Killers?* data: the ads' search terms and conversion, and the first Booksprout reviews.
2. If it sells, book 2 is **volume 2 of the same series**. That is the move with direct evidence: our own buyers, and "want more / series" is in 16 reviews.
3. If it doesn't sell, look outside this niche rather than for another twist inside it.
