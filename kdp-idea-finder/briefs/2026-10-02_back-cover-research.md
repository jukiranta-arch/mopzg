## The formula

1. **A big top line** (7 of 8): either the numbers ("ONE KILLER. 48,319 SUSPECTS. 22 CLUES.") or a hook. It is the only line many shoppers read.
2. **A short story hook** (7 of 8), 40–110 words, often in the second person ("You've been handed the guest list", "She needs you to…").
3. **How it works** (6 of 8): 3–6 icon tiles or ticks with the numbers. Darcy adds reassurance ("No trivia. No time limits. No guessing.").
4. **The same world as the front** (7 of 8): the same palette and paper, with one prop that shows the game (a register or guest list with names struck through).
5. **A closing question** in the accent colour (5 of 8).
6. **KDP's barcode**, lower right, on a white box, on every one. Nobody puts anything there.
7. **An author bio** only on 2 of 8. It isn't needed.
8. The weakest seller has the barest back: five bullets and nothing else.

## Plan for ours

**Look:** it continues the front, on the same deep purple desk. The text sits on one cream case-file sheet. The props:
- all five tokens in a row (the front shows only 1, 4 and 5, so the back completes the set);
- a torn strip of a real list page from the book, with names struck through.

**Copy draft** (about 130 words; most of it is from the book's own opening, so the voice matches):

> **NOTHING EVER HAPPENS IN JUNIPER FALLS.**
> *This autumn, it happened five times.*
>
> Five people died in five of the town's best-loved places: the bookstore, the bakery, the diner, the inn and the library. Each time, the doors were locked and every visitor's name was written down. Each time, a small numbered token was left behind, and on the back of the first one, in neat capitals: ONE OF FIVE.
>
> Five killers. One person behind them all.
>
> Sheriff Ruth Ambrose has the lists. She needs you to read them.
>
> - **5 CASES:** one evening each, with its own story, clues and list of names.
> - **8–9 CLUES A CASE:** cross out every name a clue rules out until one is left.
> - **CHECK AS YOU GO:** an answer check after every case, with no spoilers.
> - **1 MASTERMIND:** your five answers unlock the final case.
>
> **Can you find who sent the killers?**

(Juniper Falls on the back is fine: the rule is to keep it out of the title, subtitle, keywords and series name.)

**Spine** (0.320 in): "WHO SENT THE KILLERS?" in cream, with "KILLERS?" in gold, and "NORA CLEWES", on the same purple. The text runs top to bottom (the US convention), with 0.0625 in clear of each fold.

**Barcode:** a clear area of 2 × 1.2 in at the lower right. KDP prints its barcode there.
