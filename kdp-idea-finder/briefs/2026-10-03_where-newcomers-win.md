# Where self-publishing newcomers win (2026-10-03)

**Source:** 126 captured amazon.com searches, about 6,000 books.

**The measure, "newcomer wins":** how many of a search's top 16 organic books are indie, under a year old, have 150 or fewer reviews and still have a sales rank of 50,000 or better. A high count means the board is not locked by old books with thousands of reviews.

## The three patterns

1. **Seasons and trends reset the board.** In Halloween, fall and monochrome coloring, a new book reaches a rank of 2,000–9,000 with 2–19 reviews. Demand renews every season, and reviews from last year matter less.
2. **Situation gifts.** Examples: surgery recovery, hospital stays, a broken leg. The buyer is a friend or family member with an occasion, not a hobbyist comparing brands. Specific, well-made books win with about 100 reviews.
3. **Seniors and memory.** Big, steady demand. Newcomers with about 100 reviews rank around 8,000.

## Caveats

- These searches came from earlier seed lists: gifts, coloring, journals, seniors and puzzles. Other niches are simply unmeasured, not losing.
- A niche where newcomers win attracts more newcomers. The absence test must scan every captured title.
