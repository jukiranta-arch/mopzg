**18,000 SUSPECTS. 5 CASES. 1 MASTERMIND.**

Nothing ever happens in Juniper Falls. This autumn, it happened five times.

Five of the town's best-loved people died in five of its best-loved places: the bookstore, the bakery, the diner, the inn and the library. Each time, the doors were locked and every visitor's name was written down. Each time, a small numbered token was left behind. On the back of the first one, in neat capitals: ONE OF FIVE.

Five killers. One person behind them all.

Sheriff Ruth Ambrose has the lists. She needs you to read them.

**FIVE CASES, NOT ONE ENDLESS LIST**
No month-long slog through 50,000 names. Each case is its own puzzle of 2,800 to 3,700 names with 8 or 9 clues. Solve it, check it, close the file, and the next case is waiting. Do one a weekend, or all five at once.

**A REAL STORY, NOT JUST NAMES**
Every case opens with its chapter of the story: who died, where, and who was in the room. The five cases add up to one mystery, and your five answers unlock the final case: find the mastermind among 1,400 townspeople at the Town Meeting.

**ONE ANSWER, AND ONLY ONE**
Every case is tested so that exactly one name survives every clue. No second possible killer, no names the clues can't rule out.

**CHECK YOUR ANSWER WITHOUT SPOILING IT**
After each case, a quick check (the name's length and letter total) tells you if you're right without showing the name. No QR code, no website, and the full solutions stay hidden at the very back.

**EASY ON THE EYES**
Names in clean, even rows, kept well clear of the spine so nothing disappears into the fold.

**PERFECT FOR**
- fans of cozy mysteries and logic puzzles
- couples and friends looking for a screen-free evening together
- a gift for the mystery lover who has read everything
