# KDP books

Everything for researching, building and selling Amazon KDP books lives in [`kdp-idea-finder/`](kdp-idea-finder/):

- `kdpfinder/`: the KDP Capture bookmarklet and the niche and idea analysis;
- `village/`: the generator, layout and checker for *Who Sent the Killers?* (find-the-killer puzzle books);
- `whodunit/`: the first find-the-killer generator;
- `books/juniper-falls/`: the published book: interior, cover, art, A+ images and listing and ads plans;
- `briefs/`: research notes and decisions.
